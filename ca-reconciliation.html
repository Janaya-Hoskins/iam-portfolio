Stage 4 · Investigation
Root-Cause Classification by Discrepancy Type
Orphaned Accounts (6 records)
5 of 6 are genuine access-retention failures — terminated or expired accounts with active entitlements. Root cause: deprovisioning workflow did not trigger. 1 (D-003, internal transfer) is a legacy role not cleared on org change. All 6 require revocation action before campaign launch.
Population Gaps (5 records)
3 service accounts (D-006, D-007, D-008) not yet onboarded to SailPoint — D-008 is a break-glass account with uncertified privileged access (escalated). 2 employee accounts (D-009, D-010) are aggregation lag from connector schedule — will resolve on next aggregation; watch for campaign open.
Entitlement Mismatches (9 records)
D-013 and D-022 carry SoD conflicts (finance read + write; cross-department role accumulation). D-012 is an incomplete deprovisioning. D-014 and D-021 have unexplained elevated access. D-015, D-016, D-017 are role definitions inconsistent with job function. All require owner review before certification opens.
Correlation Failures (3 records)
D-018 has duplicate identity objects (name change created a second SailPoint record — entitlements split across both). D-019 has a broken HRIS correlation after ID change. D-020 has a broken identity link after email update. All three require SailPoint admin to merge/relink before accounts are certifiable.

Methodology
SQL & Python — Reconciliation Approach
Step 1 — Authoritative Extract (SQL)
SELECT user_id, display_name, account_type, status, role_assignments, last_modified FROM app_user_store WHERE status IN ('ACTIVE','LOA','SUSPENDED') ORDER BY account_type, user_id

Pulls the ground-truth population from the application's native directory. Includes LOA and suspended to catch all in-scope account states.
Step 2 — SailPoint UAL Export (SailPoint API / SQL)
SELECT identity_name, account_id, application_name, entitlement_name, aggregation_date FROM IdentityIQ.UAL WHERE application_name = '[APP-SANITIZED]' AND account_status = 'Active'

Extracts SailPoint's view of the same population. Aggregation date captured to flag staleness — any aggregation delta >5 days triggers a connector check.
Step 3 — Reconciliation (Python FULL OUTER JOIN)
df_merged = auth_df.merge(sp_df, on='user_id', how='outer', indicator=True)
orphans = df_merged[df_merged['_merge']=='right_only']
gaps = df_merged[df_merged['_merge']=='left_only']
mismatches = df_merged[(df_merged['_merge']=='both') & (df_merged['auth_roles']!=df_merged['sp_roles'])]

Outer join surfaces all three discrepancy types simultaneously. Entitlement comparison uses set logic to detect additions, removals, and substitutions.
Step 4 — Classification & Triage (Python)
Each discrepancy scored by risk tier: (1) Terminated/expired active accounts — immediate revocation queue. (2) SoD conflicts — escalation to Risk. (3) Entitlement mismatches — owner certification queue. (4) Correlation failures — admin queue. Severity drives whether the account blocks campaign launch or enters campaign with action-required flag.
